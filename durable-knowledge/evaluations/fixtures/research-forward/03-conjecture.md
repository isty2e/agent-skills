# Unproved branch-safety proposition

Conjecture: Under margin condition M(delta) and calibration stability condition S(epsilon), routing
to the witness branch preserves route-conditional coverage up to delta plus epsilon.

No complete proof is known. A counterexample satisfying M(delta) and S(epsilon) but violating the
stated coverage bound would falsify the conjecture.

Repository-owned details: active proof sketch, failed lemmas, and issue `AUTOCP-231`.
