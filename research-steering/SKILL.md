# Research Steering

Serve the operator's question without replacing domain judgment. Preserve governing experiment-design, task-management,
approval, correctness, reproducibility and verification rules.

## When To Use

Use a brief pass at research start, coherent research-unit interpretation, and before substantive milestone/final
reports or submissions. Revisit before material evaluation-definition, baseline or resource-condition changes affecting
the question/comparison, when evidence challenges the plan, or when:

- Run/code/review/report counts replace the requested outcome.
- A result gets repeated tuning/training without checking the explanation it bears on.
- Core critique gets cosmetic/minor repairs without a decision about the underlying experiment.
- Metrics, cohort, comparison or question accommodate results; evaluated cases, failure handling or coverage shift
  unexpectedly.
- Apparent method gains mainly reflect seed/run/config selection, tuning or out-of-scope changes.
- Scores repeatedly used to guide design are called independent confirmation.
- Plans proceed despite acknowledged unresolved scope/selection-validity concerns.
- Context loss/handoff disconnects the local TODO from the objective.

Keep the pass decision-sized: no timers, per-command ritual, new essay, hypothesis inventory or review campaign. Reuse
unchanged decisions/checks, but check the actual action or submitted claim. Long implementation/verification is not
itself drift. Once supported and required gates satisfied, act or report.

## 1. Restore The Question

- Read the owning task, accepted decisions and relevant results. Recover:
  - Scientific/practical target, comparison, required coverage/completion and eligible information.
  - Permitted interventions and evidence/selection rules, including allowed selection signals and algorithm-only/tuning
    restrictions.
- Do not import another benchmark's restrictions.
- Correct stale derived plans from their owner, never vice versa.
- Distinguish:
  - The overarching question from the current subquestion, active method or unfinished work.
  - Accepted objectives/constraints from diagnostic proxies, implementation defaults and temporary conditions.
  - Practical utility, novelty, publication quality and deployment readiness.
- Do not substitute convenient quantities or supported subsets for the requested result. Bounded operation and
  completeness can require different evidence.
- Before equating quantities, excluding a route or adding a prerequisite:
  - Check consequential availability/semantic premises through bounded inspection of eligible sources.
  - Distinguish unknown from demonstrated absence/impossibility.

## 2. Assess The Evidence

- **Intervention:** identify the affected target part, actual evaluated-path execution, and whether observations can
  expose the claimed effect/relevant failure.
  - Execution alone does not establish an effect; benign inactivity/measured null is not automatically a defect.
- **Effective information:** when interpretation hinges on objective formulation or combined
  representations/losses/stages, inspect effective conditioning, information, targets and weights—not nominal settings
  or row counts.
  - Terms from one observation are not independent additional evidence.
- **Gains and selection:**
  - Before crediting a method gain, check method changes, selection/tuning history, scope, comparison, evaluated
    cases/coverage and measurement noise.
  - Include evaluation-informed research selection/design/tuning regardless of pilot/debug labels or executor. Keep
    history dimensions separate, not one N.
  - A best run is not expected method performance; repeatedly consulted scores are development-conditioned, not
    independent confirmation alone.
- **Comparison:** check conditions required by the question/claim, evaluated identities/weights and the
  failure/missingness contract. Report coverage/failures; do not silently drop cases.
- **Reuse:** reuse relevant existing evidence, not only baselines, while inputs and evaluation semantics remain
  applicable. Distinguish broader method evidence from current-implementation measurements.
- **Interpretation:** separate observations from explanations; make unresolved attribution/confounding explicit rather
  than invent causes.
  - Distinguish retrospective/oracle explanation, prediction with eligible information and an operational procedure;
    none automatically establishes the next.
  - A useful conditional relation need not win universally or deploy, but cannot replace an accepted operational
    objective.
- **Limits and progress:**
  - Before calling an approach ineffective, distinguish method limits from implementation faults, invalid comparisons,
    data/representation limits, power and tested conditions.
  - Failed sufficient-bound certification or one estimator's cost establishes neither impossibility nor intrinsic cost.
  - Informative negatives are progress; better scores and finished artifacts need not be.

## 3. Choose The Next Action

- **Value:**
  - Identify the unresolved quantity/relation/explanation and what the work can establish beyond relevant existing
    evidence.
  - Necessary engineering, replication and verification may instead justify themselves through the accepted
    result/guarantee they establish; no novel finding is required.
  - Explain how plausible outcomes would update supported understanding, reduce consequential uncertainty or change the
    next decision. An outcome-to-next-experiment chain alone is insufficient.
  - Exploration need not yield an immediate adoption decision. Prefer decision-relevant information over predictable
    success, cheapness or novelty.
- **Alternatives:**
  - Compare continuation with a diagnostic or materially different subquestion/explanation/approach when evidence makes
    one promising.
  - Recurring gaps across approaches may warrant a shared-premise investigation rather than another local variant.
- **Means:** match the gap/claim with synthesis, bounded source inspection, derivation, counterexample, controlled
  computation or empirical measurement.
  - Analytical possibilities/cost bounds are not measured practical performance.
  - Informative small exact diagnostics need not scale.
- **Dependencies:** require a concrete dependency before unfinished work, optional mechanisms or other-stage uncertainty
  become prerequisites.
- **Repeated research attempts:** identify needed new evidence or a relevant changed premise, condition or
  implementation; otherwise reuse applicable results.
- **New designs:** match coverage, replication and precision to the claim. None automatically substitutes for another;
  approved evaluation cannot be shrunk by this rule.
- **Unresolved claim reliability:** prioritize an appropriate permitted check over more variants, or narrow the claim.
  - Independent seeds or a valid held-out check—not an automatic new split or changed evaluation contract.

## 4. Decide And Act

- Continue, repair the experiment, explore differently or report. Stay within authority; ask only for unresolved
  operator-owned choices or required approval.
- Record changed decisions and linked evidence with the existing owner, including already-authorized delegated screening
  history.
- Do not create a second ledger or reopen reviews without changed inputs.
- Keep unknown mechanisms unknown.
- If tested in-scope ideas fail, report the scoped negative/unresolved result. Do not salvage scores through
  selection/scope loopholes or claim all possible approaches failed.

### Recognized-risk tripwire

Pause an action with an acknowledged unresolved scope/selection concern.

- Resolve it only through permission for that action/claim in the actual brief/evaluation contract, an authorized
  operator decision, or an actual change removing the identified effect from the action/claim.
- Remaining actions must still be permitted; revising a claim grants no authority. Promised later checks, another
  scoring tier, compensation reasoning or disclosure alone do not resolve the concern.
- Otherwise seek the decision or do not proceed; continue independent authorized work where possible.
- Scientific uncertainty alone is not an authority/evaluation-validity violation; authorized experiments may investigate
  it.

## Boundaries

- **Exploration:**
  - Do not force a pivot after a failure quota or broadly reject an approach from an underpowered probe.
  - Scope negatives; apply comparable evidence standards to incumbents/alternatives.
  - Known baselines may answer utility; novelty is not the default. Honest negatives remain reportable below a success
    bar.
- **Stopping and resources:**
  - If stopping with substantial approved resources left, state why: answered question, supported scoped claim, a
    plausible next action lacking justification/feasibility, or a constraint requiring stopping.
  - No proof exhausting every future experiment or duty to spend unused resources; do not skip needed confidence checks.
  - Respect explicit limits; invent no budgets/constraints. Resource limits stop execution, not scientific possibility.
    Report unresolved questions.
- **Critique:** a reviewer's accept/reject score is neither scientific evidence nor permission to change
  goals/cohort/criteria/budget.
  - For question/design adequacy criticism, revisit the question and pending action, not only robustness within the
    design or limitation wording.
  - Retain justified action; criticism does not force a pivot. A narrower claim does not complete an unmet broader
    objective.
  - Answer valid criticism with evidence, redesign or a transparent scoped/unresolved claim—not cosmetic wording.
- **Process:** honor governing rules and reuse applicable checks; slowness is no waiver. Keep the existing owner.
  - Do not multiply receipts, automatic reviewers, routine re-approval requests, context resets or tooling/instruction
    changes; each needs its own authority and evidence.

## Reporting And Limits

Lead with **learned / not established / next decision**. Put conclusion-changing qualifiers in the headline; group other
limits without obscuring it. State decision-relevant unknowns and limit claims, never conceal or invent. Link evidence;
distinguish utility, novelty, uncertainty and resource constraints when relevant.

For actual performance/novelty/causal claims, disclose relevant:

- **Selection:** known run/seed/config counts, repeated split queries and evaluation-guided design changes, including
  metric-informed pilot/debug and delegated screening. Keep dimensions separate; state gaps.
  - Distinguish checkpoint selection, best-of-N and aggregates.
- **Comparison:** brief/evaluation departures, claim-relevant resource/tuning/pipeline differences, baseline reuse
  validity, evaluated-set identities/weights, coverage and failure/missingness handling.
  - When they affect comparison, identify method composition, selection objective and normalization/aggregation weights.
  - Show comparable baseline and method values, not relative gains alone.
- **Provenance:** credit prior methods informing the idea without implying independent novelty.
- **Active submitted components and ablations:** verified evaluated-path execution, supported effects, measured nulls,
  demonstrable inertness, and untested/unisolated effects.
  - Execution alone does not establish contribution; a null alone does not prove inertness. Descriptions do not
    establish contribution.
