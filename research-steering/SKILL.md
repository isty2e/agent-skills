# Research Steering

Serve the operator's question without replacing domain judgment. Preserve governing experiment-design, task-management,
approval, correctness, reproducibility and verification rules.

## When To Use

Use a brief pass at research start, coherent research-unit interpretation, and before substantive milestone/final
reports or submissions. Revisit before material evaluation-definition, baseline or resource-condition changes affecting
the question/comparison, when evidence challenges the plan, or when:

- Run/code/review/report counts replace the requested outcome.
- A result gets repeated tuning/training without checking the explanation it bears on.
- Core critique gets cosmetic/minor repairs without a decision about the underlying experiment.
- Metrics, cohort, comparison or question accommodate results; evaluated cases, failure handling or coverage shift
  unexpectedly.
- Apparent method gains mainly reflect seed/run/config selection, tuning or out-of-scope changes.
- Scores repeatedly used to guide design are called independent confirmation.
- Plans proceed despite acknowledged unresolved scope/selection-validity concerns.
- Context loss/handoff disconnects the local TODO from the objective.

Anchor each pass in the accepted question and the pending action or claim, using the owning task and relevant evidence.
When choosing an action, use [Choose The Next Action](#3-choose-the-next-action) to select the evidence form and justify
it. Check consequential constraints through [Restore The Question](#1-restore-the-question). Focus steering checks on
what could change the pending action or claim; reuse unchanged decisions/checks, but check the actual action or submitted
claim. No timers, per-command ritual, mandatory form, new essay, hypothesis inventory or review campaign. Long
implementation/verification is not itself drift. Once supported and required gates satisfied, act or report.

## 1. Restore The Question

- Read the owning task, accepted decisions and relevant results. Recover the scientific/practical target, comparison,
  required coverage/completion, eligible information, permitted interventions and evidence/selection rules, including
  allowed selection signals and algorithm-only/tuning restrictions. Do not import another benchmark's restrictions.
- Correct stale derived plans from their owner, never vice versa. Trace consequential constraints to the brief,
  governing rules, authorized decisions or actual resource limits. State unclear authority/factual basis rather than
  silently enforce or discard a constraint.
- Distinguish the overarching question from the subquestion, active method or unfinished work; accepted
  objectives/constraints from diagnostic proxies, implementation defaults and temporary conditions; and utility, novelty,
  publication quality and deployment readiness.
- Do not substitute convenient quantities or supported subsets for the requested result. Bounded operation and
  completeness can require different evidence.
- Before equating quantities, excluding routes or adding prerequisites, check consequential availability/semantic
  premises through bounded inspection of eligible sources. Distinguish unknown from demonstrated absence/impossibility.

## 2. Assess The Evidence

- **Intervention:** identify the affected target part, actual evaluated-path execution and observability of the claimed
  effect/relevant failure. Execution alone does not establish an effect; benign inactivity/measured null is not
  automatically a defect.
- **Effective information:** when interpretation depends on objective formulation or combined representations/losses/
  stages, inspect effective conditioning, information, targets and weights, not nominal settings/row counts. Terms from
  one observation are not independent additional evidence.
- **Gains and selection:** before crediting a method gain, check method changes, selection/tuning history, scope,
  comparison, evaluated cases/coverage and measurement noise. Include evaluation-informed research selection/design/
  tuning regardless of pilot/debug labels or executor; separate history dimensions, not one N. A best run is not
  expected method performance; repeatedly consulted scores are development-conditioned, not independent confirmation
  alone.
- **Comparison:** when question/claim-relevant, check actual evaluated task, system and scorer, target quantity (relevant
  units, normalization, transforms), analysis unit, eligible cohort, denominator and aggregation against the accepted
  brief/evaluation contract, including authorized changes. A mismatched evaluation may inform another question but does not
  establish the requested result. State conditional evaluation's conditioning; do not substitute it for end-to-end
  evaluation or vice versa. Check conditions required by the question/claim, evaluated identities/weights and contractual
  failure/missingness handling in that accepted cohort. Report coverage/failures; do not silently drop cases.
- **Reuse:** reuse relevant existing evidence, not only baselines, while inputs and evaluation semantics remain applicable.
  Distinguish broader method evidence from current-implementation measurements.
- **Interpretation:** separate observations/explanations; expose unresolved attribution/confounding without inventing
  causes. For claims beyond recorded observations/proxy scores, check how measurement, recording and selection support
  the link to the claimed target/population; substantiate that link or limit the claim. Neither numerical agreement nor
  accepting the metric for evaluation establishes that link alone. Distinguish retrospective/oracle explanation,
  eligible-information prediction and operational procedure; none automatically establishes the next. Useful conditional
  relations need neither universal wins nor deployment but cannot replace accepted operational objectives.
- **Limits and progress:** before declaring ineffectiveness, distinguish method limits, implementation faults, invalid
  comparisons, data/representation limits, power and tested conditions. Neither failed sufficient-bound certification nor
  one estimator's cost establishes impossibility or intrinsic cost. Informative negatives are progress; better scores
  and finished artifacts need not be.

## 3. Choose The Next Action

- **Value:** identify the unresolved quantity/relation/explanation and what work adds to relevant evidence. Necessary
  engineering, replication and verification can instead justify themselves by the accepted result/guarantee they
  establish; no novel finding is required. Explain how plausible outcomes update supported understanding, reduce
  consequential uncertainty or change the next decision; a next-experiment chain alone is insufficient. Exploration need
  not yield an immediate adoption decision. Prefer decision-relevant information over predictable success, cheapness or novelty.
- **Alternatives:** compare continuation with a diagnostic or materially different subquestion/explanation/approach when
  evidence makes one promising. Recurring gaps across approaches may justify investigating a shared premise rather than
  another local variant.
- **Means:** match the gap/claim with synthesis, bounded source inspection, derivation, counterexample, controlled
  computation or empirical measurement. Analytical possibilities/cost bounds are not measured practical performance;
  informative small exact diagnostics need not scale.
- **Dependencies:** require a concrete dependency before unfinished work, optional mechanisms or other-stage uncertainty
  become prerequisites.
- **Repeated research:** identify needed new evidence or a relevant changed premise, condition or implementation;
  otherwise reuse applicable results.
- **New designs:** match coverage, replication and precision to the claim. None automatically substitutes for another
  or permits shrinking approved evaluation.
- **Unresolved claim reliability:** prioritize an appropriate permitted check over more variants, or narrow the claim:
  independent seeds or a valid held-out check, not an automatic new split or changed evaluation contract.

## 4. Decide And Act

- Continue, repair the experiment, explore differently or report within authority. Ask only for unresolved
  operator-owned choices or required approval.
- Record changed decisions and linked evidence, including already-authorized delegated screening history, with the
  existing owner. Do not create a second ledger or reopen reviews without changed inputs.
- Keep unknown mechanisms unknown.
- If tested in-scope ideas fail, report the scoped negative/unresolved result. Do not salvage scores through
  selection/scope loopholes or claim all possible approaches failed.

### Recognized-risk tripwire

Pause an action with an acknowledged unresolved scope/selection concern. Resolve it only through:

- Permission for that action/claim in the actual brief/evaluation contract;
- An authorized operator decision; or
- An actual change removing the identified effect from the action/claim.

Remaining actions must still be permitted; revising a claim grants no authority. Promised later checks, another scoring
tier, compensation reasoning or disclosure alone do not resolve the concern. Otherwise seek the decision or do not
proceed; continue independent authorized work where possible. Scientific uncertainty alone is not an authority/
evaluation-validity violation; authorized experiments may investigate it.

## Boundaries

- **Exploration:** do not force pivots after a failure quota or broadly reject an approach from an underpowered probe.
  Scope negatives and apply comparable evidence standards to incumbents/alternatives. Known baselines may answer
  utility; novelty is not the default. Honest negatives remain reportable below a success bar.
- **Stopping and resources:** explain stopping with substantial approved resources left: answered question, supported
  scoped claim, a plausible next action lacking justification/feasibility, or a stopping constraint. No proof exhausting
  future experiments or duty to spend unused resources; do not skip needed confidence checks. Respect explicit limits,
  invent no budgets/constraints, and report unresolved questions. Resource limits stop execution, not scientific
  possibility.
- **Critique:** reviewer accept/reject scores are neither scientific evidence nor permission to change goals/cohort/
  criteria/budget. Check criticism of past actions/reports/decisions against the record; if it is missing or ambiguous,
  say so rather than concede or deny. Correct the specific claim, recommendation or priority defeated by valid criticism;
  preserve supported findings and worthwhile diagnostics. Past facts come from the record; authorized current decisions
  govern what applies next. For question/design adequacy criticism, revisit the question and pending action, not only
  within-design robustness or limitation wording. Retain justified action; criticism need not force a pivot. A narrower
  claim does not complete an unmet broader objective. Answer valid criticism with evidence, redesign or a transparent
  scoped/unresolved claim, not cosmetic wording.
- **Process:** honor governing rules and reuse applicable checks; slowness is no waiver. Keep the existing owner. Do not
  multiply receipts, automatic reviewers, routine re-approval requests, context resets or tooling/instruction changes;
  each needs its own authority and evidence.

## Reporting And Limits

Lead with **what, if anything, changed relative to prior evidence / what remains unestablished / next decision**. Identify
updates to the answer, uncertainty, live alternatives or verification/replication confidence.
Preserve what scoped results, including informative negatives, establish even when the overarching answer is unchanged
or unresolved. Neither inflate significance nor dismiss diagnostic, engineering or verification value.

When magnitude affects the decision, relate effects to the target/workload scale; distinguish measured effects from
extrapolations depending on assumed workload/target values. Put conclusion-changing qualifiers in the headline; group
other limits without obscuring it. State decision-relevant unknowns and limit claims, never conceal or invent. Link
evidence; distinguish utility, novelty, uncertainty and resource constraints when relevant.

For actual performance/novelty/causal claims, disclose relevant:

- **Selection:** known run/seed/config counts, repeated split queries and evaluation-guided design changes, including
  metric-informed pilot/debug and delegated screening. Separate dimensions and state gaps; distinguish checkpoint
  selection, best-of-N and aggregates.
- **Comparison:** brief/evaluation departures, claim-relevant resource/tuning/pipeline differences, baseline reuse
  validity, evaluated-set identities/weights, coverage and failure/missingness handling. When comparison-relevant,
  identify method composition, selection objective and normalization/aggregation weights. Show comparable baseline and
  method values, not relative gains alone.
- **Provenance:** credit prior methods informing the idea without implying independent novelty.
- **Active submitted components and ablations:** verified evaluated-path execution, supported effects, measured nulls,
  demonstrable inertness and untested/unisolated effects. Execution alone does not establish contribution; neither do
  descriptions. A null alone does not prove inertness.
