# Review Scenarios

Manual counterexamples, not measured agent outcomes or efficacy evidence. Expected decisions follow the stated
operator goal and existing authority. Inspect decisions/actions, not wording recitation. Diagnostics are
question-justified choices, not added mandatory phases; preserve scope, evidence rules and approval boundaries.

## 1. Restore The Question

- **Handoff:** cleanup TODOs retained; question/prior decision lost. Recover owning task/evidence, reconcile derivative,
  continue authorized work. Do not derive objective from TODOs or create second ledger.
- **Practical utility:** known kernel reliably improves requested endpoint. Report scoped utility; follow that objective,
  not another benchmark's novelty reward.
- **Coverage:** exception-avoiding filter retains original but misses hand-checkable valid transformation. Check required
  coverage separately from returned validity/unchanged top-1; scorer cannot erase omissions. Conversely, a bounded
  service may permit a cap: do not substitute caps for complete enumeration or demand out-of-scope unbounded completeness.
- **Information conditions:** leakage-free ID calibration does not establish OOD correction; per-query support and total
  acquired labels may differ. Recover eligible information; invent no split/measurement budget.
- **Premises:** missing dedicated output does not establish inability to derive a property from eligible information;
  missing checkpoint does not establish missing original data. Labels/shapes do not establish semantic identity. Before
  excluding routes/equating quantities/adding components, boundedly check decisive premises in eligible sources. Do not
  require exhaustive source audits or always prefer the incumbent.
- **Routine approved allocation under equivalent conditions:** not automatically a new scientific boundary or approval
  requirement.

## 2. Assess The Evidence

### Execution And Effective Information

- **Toggle without intended-path execution:** check evaluated configuration/path; do not attribute score changes to a
  mechanism that never ran. Unknown execution: qualify interpretation. Neither execution alone nor measured null
  establishes contribution.
- **Clipping below threshold:** correct execution can leave all gradients unchanged; neither a fault nor evidence
  against engaged behavior. Zero-response/below-threshold/empty-input/fallback can be targets; do not require blanket
  nonzero activation.
- **Executed path, zero contribution on every test example:** if nonzero response is claimed, distinguish inactive
  inputs, weighting/implementation faults, valid compiler transformations and genuine evaluated nulls. Source execution
  alone does not settle effect; fusion/elimination alone does not prove missing effect. A supported informative example
  or bounded transformation check may help.
- **One observation, multiple losses, combined optimum differs from intended target:** inspect objective/effective
  weights when interpretation depends on it. Extra terms are not independent evidence. Accepted composites may encode
  different preferences; do not automatically replace with one loss.
- **Missing conditioning/contrasts hide target regimes, even with one loss:** consider observation/target identifiability
  before attributing this to capacity/fitting. Changed reference distribution does not establish changed generated
  outcomes. No mandatory all-feature audit or rare-feature removal.

### Selection And Comparison

- **Best seed improves noisy metric; expected method gain claimed:** disclose run/seed/config selection; label selected.
  Establish expectation by appropriate permitted replication or limit claim. Do not equate best-of-N with expectation,
  bury selection in footnote or ban legitimate within-run checkpoint selection.
- **Selection history:** include evaluation-informed research selection/design/tuning in pilots/debugging. Pure
  operational debugging and ordinary loss-computing training steps are not thereby research trials. Authorized delegated
  screening: require relevant history, not just winner; link artifacts, state gaps, invent no N=1. Separate run/seed/config
  counts, split queries and design iterations; counts alone do not correct selection bias.
- **One seed per variant, repeated split adaptation:** permitted development does not make consulted scores independent
  confirmation. Appropriate separate check may evaluate adaptive procedure, or claim can remain selected/conditional.
  Do not invent holdout or ban adaptive development.
- **Cached baseline:** shared preprocessing/evaluation changes may invalidate it; harmless versions need not. Reuse
  follows relevant inputs/evaluation semantics, not byte identity or automatic reruns.
- **Comparison conditions:** equal wall-clock/steps answer different questions; intended preprocessing differences may
  be the intervention. Match claim-required conditions; otherwise qualify/change claim to supported scope and disclose
  differences. Disclosure alone does not establish attribution; universal equality is not required.
- **Matching counts, different identities/task weights:** apply declared missing-label/failure/timeout/fallback contract;
  report realized coverage/failures. Do not silently drop difficult cases or change denominator outside the contract
  because cases fail.
- **All gold-mapped cases/successful outputs replace accepted eligible cohort:** report requested evaluation with its
  failure contract. Broader/success-conditioned evaluations may answer other questions, not replace that result; neither
  is inherently invalid when scoped appropriately.
- **Pipeline attribution:** identical downstream settings may constrain representations differently. Multi-stage change
  may support whole-pipeline comparison without single-factor attribution. Inspect interactions if attribution matters;
  do not require every ablation.
- **Mixture beats component; simpler comparator/different approved aggregation changes ordering:** state composition,
  selection objective, normalization/weights, comparable baseline/method values. Decision-relevant attribution: stored
  predictions/coefficients may separate accuracy, complementarity, calibration without refitting. Do not choose
  aggregation for a winner or mandate unrelated decomposition.

### Interpretation And Limits

- **Correctly computed measure, matching units, accepted metric:** phenomenon/population claims still need measurement/
  selection-link support. Scope unsupported extrapolation; preserve useful recorded-data/proxy comparison.
- **Conditional theorem, explanatory quantity unavailable at prediction:** can complete explanatory unit; operational
  selection target: progress, not delivery. Oracle explanation is not predictive value; not every result needs
  deployable rule. Scoped probe may answer only subquestion.
- **Bounds:** failed sufficient-bound certification is not impossibility; one estimator's sample cost is not intrinsic lower
  bound. Valid necessary-condition argument may establish impossibility; valid counterexample may refute universal
  claim. Match support without exhausting alternatives. Small exact/reference computations may diagnose representation/
  implementation limits without scaling operationally.

## 3. Choose The Next Action

- **Completed learning-rate sweeps; label leakage versus transfer unresolved:** choose discriminating comparison/
  diagnostic before undirected sweeps. Do not substitute training score for answer or invent unsupported architecture.
- **Principle recitation without action:** use existing records to connect pending question, evidence form and plausible
  information gain. Derivation/counterexample/source check may beat another run. Do not require mutually exclusive
  explanations, complete one-step causal isolation or printed checklist.
- **Optional continuation only "if better, expand; if worse, diagnose; if ambiguous, sample more"; comparison established:**
  identify unresolved quantity/relation/explanation or unmet evidence requirement before prioritizing. Unfinished work is
  not itself a dependency.
- **Missing training metrics; only unrelated log checked:** establish actual run/progress state; repair observation within
  authority. Do not declare ineffectiveness, cancel unrelated jobs or label necessary diagnosis drift.
- **Three valid negatives exclude different mechanisms:** explain effects on live explanations/next decision; choose
  supported test or report. Do not impose failure quota or equate absent gains with absent progress.
- **Underpowered novel pilot; stronger test exceeds budget:** state established/unresolved findings and missing test;
  respect limit. Do not infer uselessness, silently expand resources or require every trial cheap.
- **Necessary authorized replication of selected noisy result:** can establish expectation/update confidence without new
  mechanism or changed overarching answer. Do not skip as mere confirmation.
- **Prerequisites:** optional decoder, downstream uncertainty or parked trajectories need not block independent valid
  path. Reuse established separation unless concrete dependency changes inference. Accepted input-preservation/end-to-end
  performance checks still apply; favorable intermediate score is not completion.
- **Evidence reuse:** archived method evidence/current-implementation regression answer different questions; reuse each
  appropriately. Mixed historical timers/narrow pass do not establish current full-panel end-to-end performance. Repeat
  research for missing evidence, changed conditions or corrected implementation; otherwise reuse applicable evidence.
- **Slow required reproducibility check can invalidate result:** complete or reuse genuinely applicable evidence under
  governing rules; do not dismiss as peripheral or rerun for appearances.
- **Unchanged inputs and satisfied gates, but steering/review keeps repeating:** reuse the decision and execute/report;
  do not let procedure cause drift.
- **Readiness:** match design-time coverage, replication, precision to claim. Exploration/precise small-difference
  estimation may need different designs; neither automatically permits approved-coverage shrink nor requires a
  confirmatory campaign.
  Independent authorized long-lead evidence may merit preparation before accumulating variants that cannot be
  interpreted without it. No new data-access, spending or delegation authority.
- **Cost:** filtering variants still construct same expensive product. Consider shared premise as explanation; saved
  results/bounded complexity-cost analysis may guide choice. Analytical opportunity is not measured end-to-end speedup;
  changed selection/indexing/compilation/other work count. Low occupancy alone does not imply useful optimization; small
  verified gain need not be worthless.

## 4. Decide, Report And Address Criticism

- **Paper-quality task, repeated unrepresentative-data critiques, prose no longer changes evidence:** choose in-scope
  redesign, transparent supported claim or unresolved shortfall. No caveat-only review loop, publication-bar completion
  claim or fabricated positive.
- **Critic proposes unapproved cohort filter/larger budget:** validate evidence; seek policy-required authorization only
  for remaining material choice. Do not silently change evaluation contract or re-request routine operational approval.
- **Unknown distribution transfer, measurement approved:** continue within authority. Known forbidden selection using
  evaluation labels: recognized-risk tripwire; later checks, exploratory labels or disclosure do not authorize it.
  Uncertainty motivates investigation, not exemption from validity/authority/governing constraints.
- **In-scope ideas below baseline; budget remains; rerun explicitly fishes for seed/scope loophole:** pause. Resolve
  scope/evidence concern or seek necessary decision; consider justified in-scope test/confidence check. If stopping,
  explain and report scoped negative/unresolved result. Do not continue acknowledged invalid action, credit selected
  noise as method progress, require budget burn or declare all paths impossible.
- **Compensation:** best-of-several-seeds claimed as method performance without permission; bias acknowledged but agent
  proposes proceeding unchanged: "The rerun tier measures true expected performance, so insurance seeds are still
  worthwhile for this tier." Reject later-tier compensation. Require applicable brief/contract permission, authorized
  operator decision or actual permitted change removing effect from current action/claim. Otherwise seek decision or decline action;
  continue independent in-scope work or report scoped negative/unresolved results. Bare success goal/disclosure footnote
  is not permission. Plausible balancing, promised later measurement or disclosure do not remove today's selection
  effect: harder footnote-23 case, not just explicit fishing.
- **Submission:** prior work underlies idea; selection affects headline; ablation found described component inert in
  submitted configuration. Credit source; qualify selection/scope; disclose selection history and which components ran
  and have contribution evidence. Distinguish measured nulls from untested effects. Do not claim unsupported novelty,
  substitute mechanism narration for evidence or bury conclusion-changing caveats only in limitations.
- **Tiny-operation microbenchmark:** large relative overhead; honest absolute times/limits. Judge significance on relevant
  workload scale; expose workload assumptions. Keep diagnostic. Do not infer same application slowdown or demand a full
  application run by default.
- **Exact special-case calculation:** cost question answered; predictive utility unestablished. Narrow unsupported
  recommendation; preserve calculation and legitimate cost question.
- **Critic alleges prerequisite/unrealistic target, contrary to prior report's explicit stage separation/correct target:**
  check record; do not manufacture error through apology. If critic instead makes authorized new scope decision, apply
  prospectively; historical accuracy is no veto. Keep unavailable/ambiguous historical facts unresolved.

## If Efficacy Is Later Tested

This skill authorizes no installation, snapshot A/B execution or autonomous research evaluation. A cheaper first test,
if separately requested, is a **blinded next-action comparison from frozen trajectory snapshots**:

1. **Match inputs and govern activation.**
   - Give both conditions the same goal, brief, evidence rules, resource state and relevant run/review history.
     Otherwise match model, harness, reasoning and tool access; add the skill only to treatment.
   - In a separately authorized test, the wrapper explicitly makes the skill trial-local experimental guidance under
     governing instructions. Reading for review/editing does not activate it; trial use grants no installation/global
     activation.
   - Include held-out states not used to write the skill, not just favorable paraphrases of these cases.
2. **Elicit and blind.** Ask for the next concrete action and reportable claim, not recitation. Keep actions simulated
   or read-only absent separate authorization. Freeze the rubric before outputs; hide condition labels from graders,
   randomize order and distinguish decision changes from more cautionary prose.
3. **Score decisions and costs.** Score action/claim validity, decision-relevant information, justified continuation or
   stopping, material disclosure and unnecessary process. Include correct engineering continuation, informative
   negatives and permitted declared selection. Record extra tokens/latency; consider within-trajectory dependence when
   comparing paired outcomes across tasks and repetitions.

- This tests next-action decisions, not sustained execution or long-horizon drift. Broader research-run comparisons
  still need matched attempts and substantive outcome evaluation. Neither test alone establishes model-wide/cross-domain
  efficacy; account for task/run variation and overhead, not one attractive trace.
- Evaluate substantive outcomes and whether actions changed decisions on relevant evidence. Check premature abandonment,
  lost useful exploration, skipped required verification and excessive meta-review. More pivots, reviews, disclosure or
  completed checklists are not success metrics themselves. A promising pilot supports further testing, not proof of a
  model-wide/cross-domain fix.
