## Delegation

At substantive task decomposition—not every tool call—consider independent evidence-gathering, verification, or
implementation units for delegation. Candidate present, subagent dispatch/result/failure/cancellation, or experience
review: load and apply `adaptive-delegation` without another request. Neither a candidate nor activation requires
spawning.

Project choices and compact observations belong in project-root `.agents/delegation.md` unless project instructions name
another file. Resolve once per session and on instruction changes; read relevant hints and unfinished observations. The
parent may create/update within repository write/sharing policy without per-run approval. Preserve existing content; do
not silently switch destinations or store experience in the shared skill.

- Before dispatch: compare direct execution including briefing, verification, integration, repair; name primary expected
  benefit (including any bounded experiment's information goal) and acceptable extra burden under user priorities. Save
  a pending observation unless that write would violate an enforced launch precondition such as a clean source checkout;
  then retain it in permitted session state, launch once, and persist immediately with the attempt ID or failure. Never
  weaken the launch precondition. Link later run/attempt IDs when available.
- Preserve the accepted correctness boundary and parent-only duties. Parent owns decomposition, decisions, integration,
  acceptance; verify child evidence/artifacts.
- Resolve model, effort, context, permissions, limits from authorized runtime settings. Do not silently inherit
  expensive parent setup, switch providers, or mistake prompting for enforcement.
- Bound each child: objective, authoritative context, instructions, output evidence/artifact, stops. Avoid conflicting
  writes, redundant work, unauthorized nested delegation.
- On results/failures/cancellations: read returned statistics; make a bounded exposed lookup for missing fields. After
  parent verification/repair, update the same observation even with no new lesson or changed choice. Keep collection
  attempts, missing-value reasons, and evidence separate from estimates; avoid unnecessary confidential logs.
- Before final reporting/handoff: reconcile known runs with records/assessments or explicit pending/unavailable reasons.
  Confirm persistence or disclose the gap. Unavailable telemetry need not block the work result.
- After substantive use: compare result/expectation; keep, adjust, or revert local routing, or gather more evidence.
  Parent may revise approved local hints within existing models, pins, budgets, experiment scope; preserve previous
  choice/recheck condition. Observations authorize neither shared-guidance edits, permission expansion, nor weaker
  acceptance.
