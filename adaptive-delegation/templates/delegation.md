# Project Delegation Experience

Project-scoped choices and observations, subordinate to instructions and authorized settings. This file changes no model,
permission, budget, or acceptance rule.

## Current Hints

Keep one current choice per scope and its rollback. Add a hint only when evidence warrants one; observations remain useful
when the choice does not change.

```markdown
### <Task scope and routing decision>

- Applies to: <task family and relevant model/profile, host, prompt, or skill versions>
- Choice: <current choice, intended benefit, and acceptable burden>
- Evidence: <outcomes, checks, repair, references, counterevidence, uncertainty>
- Previous choice: <rollback>
- Recheck: <trigger and evidence for keeping, revising, or reverting>
- Updated: <date and settings version>
```

## Observations

Start pending under the skill's launch-precondition rule, then update the same entry after execution and parent review.
A fanout may share one decision while identifying each child and retry. Link detailed artifacts; keep missing values and
reasons explicit.

```markdown
### <Project-unique decision ID> — <date; pending, assessed, or incomplete with reason>

- Task and choice: <scope/revision; expectation, acceptable burden, selection basis>
- Settings: <host/skill/parent; intended and effective child model, effort, context, concurrency, limits>
- Attempts: <child/run IDs, status, retries, fallbacks, cancellation, artifacts>
- Measurements: <usage, cost/quota, duration, parent burden; units, source, coverage, lookup outcome, unknown reasons>
- Artifact result: <contract satisfied, partial, incorrect, or unverified; evidence and uncertainty>
- Use: <accepted unchanged, local repair, substantial rework, discarded, or unused>
- Task acceptance: <accepted, rejected, or pending; checks and remaining gaps>
- Next: <expectation met or unknown; keep, adjust, revert, or gather evidence; hint/recheck reference>
```

Update repeated notices by identity. Preserve corrections and contrary evidence, never turn missing into zero, and keep
incomplete entries visible. Do not store credentials, unnecessary source, or transcripts, and do not share without
authority.
